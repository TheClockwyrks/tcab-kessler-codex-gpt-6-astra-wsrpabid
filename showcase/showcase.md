# Kessler

The sky is full of dead machinery. You have one deflector, a demolition ball, and a planet worth protecting. Circle the world and turn an orbital junkyard into a controlled chain reaction.

Three rings stand between you and a clean sky. The inner ring holds its ground while the outer two counter-rotate. Strike armored derelicts twice, slip through the gaps, and meet the returning ball before it falls into the atmosphere. Catch a shot near the edge of your deflector to send it away at a new angle.

Wreckage has its rewards. Catch falling salvage to widen your deflector, launch extra balls, raise a protective shield, or pierce straight through metal. Watch the red pods: their narrowing effect can turn an easy return into a desperate sweep.

Clear every derelict to earn a wave bonus and face a faster orbit. Three lives, endless waves, and a score that grows with every piece you bring down. Clear the sky. Keep the world.
